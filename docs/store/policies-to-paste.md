# Policy fixes to paste (Shopify → Settings → Policies)

The connection used to build this store can't edit policies, so these two
changes need pasting by hand. Terms of service, Privacy policy, Contact
information and the Refund policy are already correct and unchanged.

## 1. Shipping policy — replace the whole "Order Processing" section

Find the heading **Order Processing** and its two paragraphs ("...processed within 1
business day..." and "Orders placed after noon..."), and replace them with:

> **Order Processing**
>
> All IZAVEN products are made to order. Your order is produced after you place it,
> so production takes longer than for items shipped from stock. For most
> destinations the total time from order to delivery is typically 12–35 business
> days, depending on where you live.
>
> You'll receive a confirmation email with tracking as soon as your order ships, if
> the shipping method supports it.

Then, under **Shipping Methods & Delivery Times**, replace the first paragraph with:

> Available shipping methods and prices are shown at checkout. Delivery times are
> estimates, not guarantees. They may be affected by carrier delays, weather, or
> other events outside our control.

Why: the old text said orders ship within 1 business day, which isn't true for
made-to-order products. The "12–35 business days" figure comes from your own Printful
shipping profile; check it against Printful's current estimates before publishing.

## 2. Legal notice — fill in your details

The notice currently shows unfilled placeholders. Replace the three placeholder
blocks with your real details (delete any that don't apply):

- **Operated by:** your full legal name or company name
- **NIP, REGON, registration (CEIDG/KRS):** only if you're a registered business
- **Phone:** optional

If you're not registered yet, simply delete the "Operated by", NIP/REGON/Registered
lines and the Phone line, and keep your name in "Store operator".
